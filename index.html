About

The designer
behind the work

I trained as a painter and stone sculptor at UNAM's National School of Fine Arts, and in 1999 I moved into digital, back when websites were built with tables and Flash. Since then I have designed for newspapers, TV, government, logistics, agriculture and smart homes, and every project taught me a new industry from scratch.

Sculpting in stone teaches you to look for the shape that is already inside the block, and that is still how I work: I listen to users and stakeholders until I understand the structure of the problem, and only then I start removing what is not needed.

I hold a Bachelor in Visual Arts from UNAM and the Professional Scrum™ with UX certification, and lately I use AI to turn mockups into functional prototypes and design systems. Outside of work I travel whenever I can, from Tokyo to Budapest, and I am going back to my roots with 3D sculpting and printing.
